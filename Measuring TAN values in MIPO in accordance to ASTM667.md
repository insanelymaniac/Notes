#autotitrator  
So this is Kong's method to measure TAN of samples in accordance to ASTM667. For use internally in MIPO. 

# Solvent Preparation 
The solvent is a mixture of 50/50/0.5 vol% toluene IPA water. Mix in bottle and shake thoroughly until it is not cloudy. 
	
# Electrode Preparation 
Electrode should be stored long term in electrolyte solution. Before use, check the glass bulb for particles stuck on it. Wipe down with kimwipe until the electrode looks clear and smooth. Before use, condition in DI water for 5 minutes to hydrate glass membrane. 

# Titrant Preparation 
Dissolve 1 to 2 small pellets of NaOH in a little bit of water, then add to 1L of IPA solution. Stir overnight. Keep it stirring when not in use to maintain solubility. If precipitate is formed (due to CO2 in the air), filter the titrant. 
# Autotitrator Preparation 
Ensure that the burette is filled with the titrant. If filled with DI water, flush out DI water to replace with titrant. This may require up to 10 flushes (judge until  no significant phase difference in observed). If already filled with titrant, cycle the titrant burette mixture twice to ensure homogeneity. 

# SOP for titration 
1) Complete all preparation steps mentioned above. 
2) In a 250mL beaker, dissolve between 0.1g (for TAN >50) to 10g (for TAN < 5) of sample in the solvent. Measure accurately the quantity added. For calibration, use around 0.5g of KHP in ultrapure water. 
3) Sonicate until completely dissolved. For MFO, some components will not dissolve properly, so stir the sample/solvent mixture for 20 minutes on a stir plate to ensure complete suspension. 
4) Titrate the sample.
