# How to detect acetic acid with GC-MS column HP-5MS

from https://pubs.acs.org/enfuem/article/31/9/9455/753815/pH-Neutralization-of-Aqueous-Bio-Oil-from

Relevant Passage : "The GC-MS analysis was also performed at the BioAnalytical Mass Spectrometry Facility of the Georgia Institute of Technology. Details of this GC-MS analysis are also available in the literature. (6) The GC-MS was equipped with an Agilent DB-5 column (30-m long by 0.25 mm i.d. with a coating thickness of 0.25 μm). The program of the analysis began with the oven at 35 °C, and this temperature was held for 1 min. Then, it was raised to 300 °C at a heating rate of 15 °C/min, and held at 300 °C for 6.3 min. Helium was used as the carrier gas, and samples were prepared with methanol. A sample of 1 μL was injected with a 50:1 ratio split mode, and the inlet temperature was 280 °C. The chemicals were identified by comparing the mass spectra obtained with data from the NIST library using criteria of NIST match score/mass fragments and retention time."

DB-5 column is equivalent to HP-5 column
