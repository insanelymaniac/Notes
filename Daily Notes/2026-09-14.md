Experimental plan for standardizing TAN measurements in potentiometric titration: 
Theoretical basis: the main theoretical basis lies in the fact that 0mV in potentiometric titration corresponds to a pH of 7. In most oils, we expect that the endpoint mV to be negative (lower H+ concentration in solution than in standard electrolyte) due most acidic compounds present being weakly acidic compared to the titrant, a strong base. 

How to test: to compare various feedstocks against a stock standard solution, and to plot dU/dV, analyse the steepest part midpoint as the endpoint.
