Something I saw and thought 
If there exist $n$ positive reals $a_i (i = 1,2,3,...,n)$ 
$$
\sum_{i= 1}^{n} a_i = 1
$$
For all positive integers $n$
Show that for all positive integers $k$, 
$$
\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^k \geq \frac{1}{n^{k-1}}
$$
How to prove?Basic QMAM 

$$
\sqrt[k]{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^k}{n}} \geq \frac{\sum_{i= 1}^{n} a_i}{n} = \frac{1}{n}
$$
Then it becomes how to prove QMAM
